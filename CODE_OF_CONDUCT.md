# CartFlow Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The CartFlow project is built on the belief that great software comes from inclusive, respectful communities. Everyone who participates — whether reporting a bug, reviewing a PR, answering questions, or writing code — deserves a harassment-free experience.

We commit to maintaining a space that is welcoming regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

---

## Standards We Uphold

Behaviors that strengthen the community:

- Listening actively and assuming good intent
- Giving feedback that is honest, kind, and actionable
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and moving forward
- Prioritizing the long-term health of the project over individual preferences

Behaviors that are not acceptable:

- Sexual or discriminatory language and imagery
- Personal attacks, insults, or political provocation
- Harassment in any form, public or private
- Sharing someone's private information without their explicit consent
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would find threatening or unwelcoming

---

## Responsibilities of Project Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for defining and upholding these standards. They have the authority — and the obligation — to remove, edit, or reject contributions (comments, commits, issues, PRs) that violate this code, and to explain their decisions transparently.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies inside all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channels. It also applies when someone is representing CartFlow in public — for example at a conference, on social media, or via an official account.

---

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code, report it to the maintainers at **support@evershop.io**. All reports are treated confidentially, reviewed promptly, and handled fairly. Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation.

---

## Enforcement Tiers

### 1. Correction

**Situation:** A one-off lapse in professionalism — inappropriate language, an unconstructive comment.  
**Response:** A private message from a maintainer explaining what happened and why it was a problem. A public acknowledgment may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Situation:** A pattern of minor violations or a more serious single incident.  
**Response:** A formal warning with defined consequences for repetition. Continued interaction with those affected — including unsolicited messages — is prohibited for a defined period. Violations of the warning terms escalate to a temporary ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Situation:** A serious or sustained breach of community norms.  
**Response:** A time-limited ban from all community interaction. No public or private contact with involved parties during the ban period.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Situation:** A clear pattern of harmful behavior, targeted harassment, or aggression toward groups of individuals.  
**Response:** Permanent removal from all CartFlow community spaces.
